About Me

Hi, I'm Pixideus but call me Pixi ! Pixideus was my first character (mage) when i started playing WoW back in 2005.

I've been raiding and chasing server first until mid-Cataclysm. I'm now chasing decor instead of bosses :) Housing has completely changed my vision of the game, I'm having so much fun. I enjoy transforming empty spaces into immersive environments inspired by fantasy, architecture, films and games.

I don't pretend to be better than anyone doing housing, i only have ideas, a lot of ideas !

I often get asked "How did you make this?" and I really appreciate the interest! I may share some tips and explanations along the way, but I don't want tutorials to become a second job. :) I mainly play after work and on weekends. Making videos from time to time is something I enjoy, but creating detailed tutorials takes a lot of time. When I have a few hours to play, I'd rather spend that time playing WoW and building than making tutos.

Want to visit one of my houses on the EU servers? Have a question about a build, or simply want to say hello?

Feel free to reach out through any of my socials. (link below) You can also send a message ingame (EU Pixideus-Vol'jin)

Blueprints

Housing is a way of self-expression, and turning my builds into products would remove a lot of the fun for me. I'm still thinking about how I want to approach blueprints, but I don't want my builds to end up on shady sales websites, with someone else taking credit for my work.

I will share my builds but not systematically, not always, not to anyone. I already had demands in the past for some of my builds, like the house inside the tree. Such creations are more elaborate, they took many hours of design, golds, testing and tearing my hear out! Contact me and we'll talk about it :)

Tipping could be considered as well : if i build a lot, i don't have time to farm mats and golds, and a tip could help me buy more decor and create more crazy builds! And think that you will owe a room that took 30 hours to create ! And be respectful towards other's creations, give them credit if you share :)

I may also create a Ko-Fi page for those who would like to support me. But, for me, World of Warcraft and especially Housing is a passion and a way to relax — it's not my job or a "business". Turning it into one would make it feel like work, and that would take away from what I enjoy about it.. My goal has always been to share ideas with the community while keeping that passion alive.

My speech and thoughts may evolve over time and i will keep you updated on socials, it's too early to have a fixed opinion as i don't know how much or if people would ever be interested with my blueprints !
